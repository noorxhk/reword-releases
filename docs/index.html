Features

Everything between your voice and the send button

Dictate anywhere

Hold fn, speak, let go. Your words appear where the cursor is. Double-tap fn to go hands-free.

Cleans as you speak

Filler words, false starts and “2pm, actually 3pm” are fixed for you. Spoken lists turn into real lists.

Rewrite anything

Select text and press ⌥2: rephrase, shorten, make it formal, fix grammar or change the tone.

Urdu ↔ English

Speak Urdu, send English. Or turn Roman Urdu into proper Urdu script. Hindi works too.

Speak a command

Select text, hold fn + ⌃ and say what you want: “make it polite and shorter”.

Learns your words

A dictionary that picks up names and terms you fix, snippets for things you type often, and a style per app.

FAQ

Before you install

Is Reword really free?

Yes. Dictation is free and runs on your Mac. For cleanup and rewriting, you add a free key from Groq, Google Gemini or OpenRouter. It takes a minute, and the install guide shows how.

Where does my voice go?

Nowhere. Your speech is turned into text on your Mac. When cleanup is on, only that text is sent to the AI service you chose. Your key is kept in your Mac's Keychain.

Why does macOS say it can't open Reword?

Reword isn't from the App Store, so the first time macOS asks you to confirm. Go to System Settings › Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway. You only do this once. The one-line Terminal install skips this step.

Which languages does it understand?

English, Urdu, Roman Urdu and Hindi, including mixed sentences. Pick “Urdu + English” in Settings › Voice if you switch between them.

Which Macs does it run on?

Any Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or newer) on macOS 26 Tahoe or later.

Why does it need Accessibility permission?

To type the text where your cursor is and to read the text you select for rewriting. It doesn't record your screen.
