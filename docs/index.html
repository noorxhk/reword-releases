FAQ

Before you install

Is Reword really free?

Yes. Dictation is free and runs on your Mac. For cleanup and rewriting you add a free key from Groq, Google Gemini or OpenRouter. It takes a minute, and the install guide shows how.

Where does my voice go?

Nowhere. Your speech is turned into text on your Mac. When cleanup is on, only that text goes to the AI service you chose, with your own key, which stays in your Mac's Keychain.

Why does macOS say it can't open Reword?

Reword isn't from the App Store, so the first time macOS asks you to confirm. Go to System Settings › Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway. You only do this once. The one-line Terminal install skips it.

Which languages does it understand?

English, Urdu, Roman Urdu and Hindi, including sentences that mix them. Pick “Urdu + English” in Settings › Voice if you switch between them.

Which Macs does it run on?

Any Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or newer) on macOS 26 Tahoe or later.

Why does it need Accessibility permission?

To type text where your cursor is, and to read the text you select for rewriting. It doesn't record your screen.
